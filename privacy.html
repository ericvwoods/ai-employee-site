Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

About This Application

Daily Coffee Brief is a privately operated personal productivity application that prepares daily summaries. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Account Data

Daily Coffee Brief accesses Google Calendar information to include calendar events in daily briefings.

It accesses YouTube account information, including playlists and associated video information, to prepare personalized YouTube summaries.

How Information Is Used

Google account information is used to generate the application's personal productivity reports. The application uses AI services to process and summarize relevant information.

Data Storage and Sharing

The application runs on a privately managed computer. Google authorization credentials are stored locally.

Information may be transmitted to configured AI services for summarization and to configured messaging services for delivery of reports.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local application data and authorization credentials can be deleted by the application operator. Google account access can also be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Future Applications

This policy covers Daily Coffee Brief. Additional applications will be identified here with their relevant data practices as they are introduced.
